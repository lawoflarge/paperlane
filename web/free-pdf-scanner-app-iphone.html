The five ways a free scanner stops being free

  • A watermark on exportThe scan is free, the clean file is not. You find out when the PDF is already in someone else's inbox.
  • A page or document capThree pages per document, or five documents in total, and the fourth asks for money.
  • Recognition behind a trialThe scan works, but search and text export are locked, which is usually the reason you installed a scanner rather than using Notes.
  • An account you did not ask forSign in to continue, and your documents are now on someone's server. For payslips and identity documents that is a real decision, not a formality.
  • A trial that renewsSeven days free, then a yearly charge. Perfectly legal, easy to miss, and the reason so many people search for scanners with a one time price.

Test any scanner in sixty seconds

  1. Scan one page and export it. Open the PDF and look at the corners and the footer for a watermark.
  2. Scan four more. Caps and paywalls usually appear between the third and the sixth document.
  3. Search inside the exported PDF. Open it in Files and search for a word you can see on the page. Nothing highlights means there is no text layer.
  4. Turn on airplane mode and scan again. If it stalls, the app is doing work on a server, which means your page went there.
  5. Read the In App Purchases list on the App Store page. A row that says per month or per year is a subscription, whatever the screenshots promise.
