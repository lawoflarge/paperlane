Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 June 2026

ScanLite is built to be private by default. This policy explains exactly what is and is not collected.

Your documents stay on your device

Scanning, edge detection, perspective correction, OCR and PDF creation all happen on your iPhone using Apple's on-device frameworks. Your scanned documents and their contents are never uploaded, never sent to us, and never leave your device. We do not have access to them and we do not collect them.

No account

ScanLite has no login or account. We do not collect your name, email, or contacts.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows banner ads via Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob may process a device identifier (such as the Identifier for Advertisers) and product-interaction data. With your permission (via the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt and, in the EEA/UK, a consent message), this data may be used to personalise ads and measure their performance — this constitutes “tracking” under Apple's definition. You can decline; the app works fully either way, and you can change your choice in iOS Settings.

The Pro version removes all ads and performs no ad-related data processing.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

What we collect

  • Scanned documents / content: not collected (processed on-device only).
  • Device identifier & product interaction: used by AdMob for third-party advertising in the free version, only with consent.
  • Personal information, contacts, location: not collected.

Children

ScanLite is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected here with a new date.
