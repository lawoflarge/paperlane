A CamScanner Alternative for iPhone, Without the Watermark

A watermark on an export is a pricing decision, not a technical limit. Here is what actually changes when you leave a cloud scanner for one that never uploads anything.

Why free scanners watermark at all

A watermark is the most efficient advertisement ever invented for this category. It rides along on a document you send to a landlord, an employer or an insurer, it is seen by people who were not looking for a scanner, and it keeps working for as long as the file exists. It costs the developer nothing and costs you a small amount of credibility on every document you send.

The alternative is to charge for the app, or to put advertising inside it. Paperlane takes the second route: no export from any tier carries a mark, and the free version is paid for by ads that live in the app and never touch the file. A banner sits under the library, and a full screen ad can appear when you close a document, never in your first session and never more often than once every three minutes. Google's consent form asks first, Ad privacy in the settings lets you change your answer, and the one time Pro purchase removes advertising entirely.

What a cloud scanner does that this one cannot

Leaving a server based scanner has real costs. They should be on the table before you install anything.

  • Your documents on every deviceA cloud library follows you to a tablet, a second phone and a browser. Paperlane is iPhone only, with no sync and no web access.
  • A share link instead of a fileCloud scanners can hand someone a link that stays current. Here you share a file through the iOS share sheet, and that is the end of it.
  • AndroidThere is no Android version and none planned in this repository.
  • Recovery when the phone is goneA server keeps a copy. Local storage does not. Your device backup is the only safety net, so make sure there is one.
  • Server side recognitionCloud OCR can support scripts and languages your iPhone does not. Paperlane uses Apple's on device recognition, so its language coverage is whatever your iOS version provides.

What you get in exchange

  • No watermark on any export, on the free tier or the paid one.
  • No page cap and no scan cap.
  • No account and no sign in. There is nothing to be locked out of.
  • No upload. Capture, recognition and PDF creation all run on the iPhone.

  • No subscription. The Pro toolkit is $4.99 once and never renews.
  • An optional Face ID or passcode lock on the library.
  • Full function in airplane mode.

Before you delete the old app

Export first. Paperlane cannot import an existing PDF library. It builds documents from what you scan and from photos in your photo library. So pull anything you care about out of the old app and store it somewhere you control, then start scanning new paper here. Deleting a cloud scanner also removes your access to whatever is still sitting on its servers.
