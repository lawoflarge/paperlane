# Paperlane Pricing

Paperlane: Scan to PDF for iOS (iPhone).

## Free
- Price: Free (no subscription, no recurring charge).
- Includes: unlimited scans, automatic edge detection and perspective correction, real multi-page PDF export, a searchable on-device text layer on every scan, full-text library search, folders and favourites, page reordering, ID mode, an optional Face ID lock, share sheet, and a local on-device library.
- No watermark, no scan limit, no account, no data collection. Supported by ads from Google AdMob, with consent asked first.

## Paperlane Pro
- Price: USD 4.99 in the United States, a one-time purchase (non-consumable). No subscription, no recurring charge. Other regions are priced in local currency.
- Unlocks: export the recognized text as .txt or .docx, smart rename, combine documents into one PDF, export as JPG or PNG, compress PDFs, markup and sign a page, save a business card as a contact, password-protect PDFs, and removes ads.

## Notes
- Scanning to PDF is always free; Paperlane Pro is optional.
- Paperlane Pro is a single purchase you keep forever, including across reinstalls.
- The App Store shows local-currency equivalents by region; USD 4.99 is the United States price.
- Source of truth: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/paperlane-scan-to-pdf/id6780053638
