# ScanLite Pricing

ScanLite: Scan to PDF — iOS (iPhone).

## Free
- Price: Free (no subscription, no recurring charge).
- Includes: unlimited scans, automatic edge detection and perspective correction, real multi-page PDF export, share sheet, and a local on-device library.
- No watermark, no scan limit, no account, no data collection.

## ScanLite Pro
- Price: EUR 4.99 — one-time purchase (non-consumable). No subscription, no recurring charge.
- Unlocks: searchable OCR text layer, combine/merge pages into one PDF, export as JPG or PNG, password-protect PDFs, and removes ads.

## Notes
- Scanning to PDF is always free; ScanLite Pro is optional.
- ScanLite Pro is a single purchase you keep forever, including across reinstalls.
- The App Store shows local-currency equivalents by region.
- Source of truth: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/scanlite-scan-to-pdf/id6780053638
