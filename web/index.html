What one scan actually produces

Hold the iPhone over a page. Apple VisionKit finds the edges and straightens the perspective, you pick a page mode, and the save writes a real multi page PDF. On the way in, the pages are read on the device, so the file you get back can be searched by the words printed on it.

  • A real PDFMulti page, correctly ordered, and readable in any PDF viewer. Not an image dressed up as a document, and never stamped with a watermark.
  • A searchable text layerWritten on every scan, at no cost. The recognized words sit invisibly over the page image, so search and text selection work in Files, Preview, Mail and anywhere else.
  • A name you did not typeThe first line the app recognizes becomes the document name, with the date after it. A receipt usually names itself after the shop.
  • A private fileIt is written to a local library on the iPhone. No server receives it, because Paperlane does not have one.

Questions

Is there a free way to scan documents to PDF on iPhone?

Yes. Paperlane scans to real, multi page PDFs for free, with no watermark, no page cap and no account. Edge detection and perspective correction come from Apple VisionKit, the same engine behind the system scanner. A one time Paperlane Pro purchase adds a toolkit on top, but scanning and PDF export stay free.

Does Paperlane put a watermark on my PDFs?

No. No tier of Paperlane stamps anything onto a page. Every scan exports as a clean multi page PDF that opens, prints and shares anywhere. The free version is paid for by ads inside the app instead, so the file you send carries no advertising and no branding.

Does Paperlane show ads?

The free version does. There is a banner at the bottom of the library, and a full screen ad can appear when you close a document, never in your first session and never more often than once every three minutes. Google's consent form asks you before the first ad loads, and you can change that answer later under Ad privacy in the settings. The ads never see your documents. Paperlane Pro removes all advertising.

How much does Paperlane cost?

Downloading and scanning are free. Paperlane Pro is a single purchase of $4.99 in the United States, priced in local currency elsewhere. It is a non subscription, non consumable in app purchase, so it does not renew, and it restores on any iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID.

Are my scans uploaded anywhere?

No. Paperlane has no servers, no account and no cloud sync. Capture, text recognition and PDF creation all run on the iPhone, and documents are stored in a local library. Because nothing is uploaded, the app works the same in airplane mode.

Are the PDFs searchable?

Yes, and that part is free. When a scan is saved, Apple's on device Vision framework reads the pages and writes an invisible text layer into the PDF, so search and text selection work in any PDF reader. Library search reads that text too, which means a word from inside a document finds the document.

Can I get the text out as a file?

Yes, with Paperlane Pro. It can copy the recognized text, export it as a .txt file, or export it as a .docx that opens in Word and Pages. The recognition itself already happened on the device when you saved the scan.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Scanning, recognition, PDF creation, search and export all run offline. An internet connection is only needed to load ads in the free version and to complete a purchase.
