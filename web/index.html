Questions

Is there a free scan to PDF app for iPhone?

Yes. Paperlane is a free iPhone app that scans documents into real, multi-page PDFs with no watermark, no scan limit, and no account. It uses Apple's VisionKit for automatic edge detection and perspective correction. An optional one-time Paperlane Pro purchase adds extra tools, but scanning to PDF stays free forever.

Does Paperlane work offline?

Yes. Paperlane runs entirely on your iPhone and needs no internet connection to scan documents, create PDFs, or browse your library. It has no servers and no account, so every file is processed and stored on-device. Because nothing is ever uploaded, Paperlane works the same in airplane mode as it does online.

Does Paperlane add a watermark to scanned PDFs?

No. Paperlane never watermarks your PDFs, even on the free tier. Every scan exports as a clean, real, multi-page PDF that opens, prints, and shares anywhere. Many free scanner apps stamp a watermark or cap pages until you subscribe; Paperlane does neither, and there is no subscription at all.

Does Paperlane show ads?

The free version shows ads, served by Google AdMob, and asks for your consent before the first one loads. You can decline, and you can change that choice later under Ad privacy in the settings. The one-time Paperlane Pro unlock removes all advertising. Ads never see your scans: scanning, OCR and PDF creation stay on your device.

How much does Paperlane cost?

Paperlane is free to download and free to scan documents to PDF, with no subscription and no recurring charge. Paperlane Pro is a single one-time purchase of EUR 4.99 that unlocks searchable OCR, page merging, JPG and PNG export, and password protection. You pay once and keep Pro forever, including across reinstalls.

Are my scanned documents private with Paperlane?

Yes. Paperlane is private by design: it has no servers, no account, and no cloud sync, so your scans never leave your iPhone. Documents live in a local, on-device library that only you control. Because there is no upload of your scans, no one but you can open your documents.

Can Paperlane create a searchable PDF with OCR?

Yes. Paperlane Pro adds an on-device OCR text layer that makes scanned PDFs searchable, so you can find any word inside a scan and select or copy its text. The OCR runs locally with Apple's Vision framework, so documents are never uploaded. OCR is part of the one-time Paperlane Pro unlock.
